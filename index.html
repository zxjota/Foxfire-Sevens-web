1. Introduction

Thank you for using Foxfire Sevens (the "App," "Service," or "we"). We built this App to deliver a great experience while respecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By downloading, installing, or using Foxfire Sevens, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

In short: We only collect what we need to run and improve the App. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your data for unrelated advertising purposes.

2. Information We Collect

We collect different kinds of information depending on how you interact with the App.

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Account information: If the App offers account creation, we may collect your email address, display name, and a securely stored password hash.
  • Support communications: When you contact us, we receive your name, email address, and the contents of your message.
  • User content: Any content you choose to create, upload, or share within the App.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device information: Device model, operating system and version, language setting, and unique device identifiers.
  • Usage data: Which features you use, session length, screen views, taps and interactions, and crash reports.
  • Network information: IP address, mobile carrier, and connection type, used for diagnostics and security.

2.3 Information we do not collect

Unless explicitly stated, we do not collect your contacts, photos, precise location, microphone, or camera without a clear notice and your permission at the moment of access.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App's core features;
  • To authenticate your identity and keep your account secure;
  • To diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve performance;
  • To personalize your in-app experience and remember your preferences;
  • To send important notices, such as updates or security alerts;
  • To respond to your support requests and communications;
  • To enforce our Terms of Service and protect against fraud or abuse.

We do not use your personal information to build advertising profiles, and we do not engage in cross-app tracking for ad targeting.

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this policy:

When retention is no longer necessary, we delete or anonymize the data securely.
